## 📑 Core Topics Covered

1. **Introduction & Decomposition Models:** Additive vs. multiplicative decomposition, log transformation, components of time series ($T, S, C, I$).
2. **Forecasting Foundations & Taxonomy:** Qualitative vs. quantitative methodologies, planning horizons (ST, MT, LT), and trend equations (linear, quadratic, exponential, $\AR(p)$).
3. **Smoothing & Moving Averages:** Simple, centered, and weighted moving average series with empirical Excel implementations.
4. **Lecture Notes:** Scanned classroom lectures covering temporal statistical concepts.
