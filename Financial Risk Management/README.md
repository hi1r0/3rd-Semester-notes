## 📑 Core Topics Covered

1. **Taxonomy of Financial Risks:** Market risk, credit risk, liquidity risk, operational risk, model risk, and legal/regulatory risk.
2. **Risk Measurement Metrics:** Volatility ($\sigma$), Value at Risk ($\VaR$), Expected Shortfall ($\ES$ / Conditional VaR), stress testing, and scenario analysis.
3. **Regulatory & Capital Frameworks:** Basel Accords (Basel I, II, III), minimum capital adequacy ratios (CAR), and risk-weighted assets (RWA).
4. **Hedging & Mitigation Instruments:** Derivative hedging (futures, forwards, swaps, options) and structural portfolio risk controls.
