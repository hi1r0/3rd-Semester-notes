\end{itemize}

\vspace{0.25em}
\noindent\textbf{\small Course Outcomes (COs):}
\begin{description}[leftmargin=1.1cm, labelsep=0.3em, itemsep=0.5pt, topsep=1pt, parsep=0pt, font=\normalfont\bfseries\footnotesize]
    \footnotesize
    \item[CO1:] Analyse how futures and forward markets operate and be able to calculate its theoretical prices and values
    \item[CO2:] Analyse the sources of financial risk and the importance of implementing effective financial risk management
    \item[CO3:] Evaluate hedging strategies using forwards, futures, options and swaps

    \item[CO5:] To evaluate the outcomes of strategies to manage Risk with the help of case Studies

\noindent\textbf{\footnotesize Unit I: Introduction to Risk Management}\\
\footnotesize
Introduction to risk management --- Sources of risk --- Risks of commodity investment --- Risks of equity investment --- Risks of fixed income investment --- Risks of currency investment --- Market risk measurement.

\vspace{0.2em}
\noindent\textbf{\footnotesize Unit II: VAR Methods and Hedging}\\
\footnotesize
An overview of VAR --- Definition, downside VAR --- VAR methods: VAR local and full valuation, delta normal methods, historical Simulation, Monte Carlo simulation --- VAR applications --- Stress testing --- Hedging: linear risk, optimal hedging, hedge ratio as regression coefficient, non-linear risk hedging.

\vspace{0.2em}
\noindent\textbf{\footnotesize Unit III: Credit Risk Management}\\
\footnotesize
Introduction to credit risk --- Measuring credit risk --- Credit exposure --- Types of credit derivatives --- Credit default swap --- Pricing and hedging credit derivatives --- Credit risk models --- Basel accord --- The Basel market risk charges, Settlement risk.

\vspace{0.2em}
\noindent\textbf{\footnotesize Unit IV: Management of Financial Risk}\\
\footnotesize
Measuring \& Managing Interest Rate Risks: DCF, TVM, YTM, Fixed Term Annuities \& PVBP --- Measuring \& Managing Market Risk: Risk/Return Trade-off, Systematic Risk, Idiosyncratic Risk, Modern Portfolio Theory \& RAROC --- Measuring \& Managing Liquidity Risk: Cash flow forecasting, Working capital management.

\vspace{0.2em}
\noindent\textbf{\footnotesize Unit V: Operation \& Integrated Risk Management}\\
\footnotesize
Measuring \& Managing Operational Risk: Brainstorming, SWOT, RCA \& Assumption Analysis --- Measuring \& Managing Credit \& Counterparty Risk: Syndication of Credit, Netting of Payments (Cross Collateralization), Guarantees \& Credit Derivatives --- Measuring Systemic \& Sovereign Risk: Debt/GDP, Debt Servicing, BoP \& FOREX Reserves.
