\noindent\textbf{Course Objectives:}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em, label=$\bullet$, noitemsep, topsep=1pt, parsep=1pt]
    \item \textit{This paper focuses the basic concept of risk management and expose various types of risk faced.}
    \item \textit{It helps to take positions for investing and trading in options and future market.}
\end{itemize}

\vspace{0.4em}
\noindent\textbf{Course Outcomes (COs):}
\begin{description}[leftmargin=1.4cm, labelsep=0.4em, itemsep=1pt, topsep=1pt, parsep=0pt, font=\normalfont\textbf]
    \item[CO1:] Analyse how futures and forward markets operate and be able to calculate its theoretical prices and values
    \item[CO2:] Analyse the sources of financial risk and the importance of implementing effective financial risk management
    \item[CO3:] Evaluate hedging strategies using forwards, futures, options and swaps
    \item[CO4:] To identify financial risks in currencies, interest rates, commodities and shares
    \item[CO5:] To evaluate the outcomes of strategies to manage Risk with the help of case Studies
\end{description}

\vspace{0.4em}
\noindent\textbf{Unit I: Introduction to Risk Management}\\
\small
Introduction to risk management --- Sources of risk --- Risks of commodity investment --- Risks of equity investment --- Risks of fixed income investment --- Risks of currency investment --- Market risk measurement.
\normalsize

\vspace{0.3em}
\noindent\textbf{Unit II: VAR Methods and Hedging}\\
\small
An overview of VAR --- Definition, downside VAR --- VAR methods: VAR local and full valuation, delta normal methods, historical Simulation, Monte Carlo simulation --- VAR applications --- Stress testing --- Hedging: linear risk, optimal hedging, hedge ratio as regression coefficient, non-linear risk hedging.
\normalsize

\vspace{0.3em}
\noindent\textbf{Unit III: Credit Risk Management}\\
\small
Introduction to credit risk --- Measuring credit risk --- Credit exposure --- Types of credit derivatives --- Credit default swap --- Pricing and hedging credit derivatives --- Credit risk models --- Basel accord --- The Basel market risk charges, Settlement risk.
\normalsize

\vspace{0.3em}
\noindent\textbf{Unit IV: Management of Financial Risk}\\
\small
Measuring \& Managing Interest Rate Risks: Discounted Cash Flow, Time Value of Money, Yield to Maturity, Fixed Term Annuities \& Present Value of Basis Point --- Measuring \& Managing Market Risk: Risk/Return Trade-off, Systematic Risk, Idiosyncratic Risk, Modern Portfolio Theory \& Risk Adjusted Return on Capital (RAROC) --- Measuring \& Managing Liquidity Risk: Forecasting cash requirements from Cash Flow Analysis, Working Capital Management.
\normalsize

\vspace{0.3em}
\noindent\textbf{Unit V: Operation \& Integrated Risk Management}\\
\small
Measuring \& Managing Operational Risk: Brainstorming, SWOT, Root Cause Analysis \& Assumption Analysis --- Measuring \& Managing Credit \& Counterparty Risk: Syndication of Credit, Netting of Payments (Cross Collateralization), Third Party Guarantees (Banks) \& Credit Derivatives --- Measuring Systemic \& Sovereign Risk: Assessing Debt/GDP, Debt/Revenue, Debt Servicing, BoP Position and FOREX Reserves \& Demographic Profile.
\normalsize
