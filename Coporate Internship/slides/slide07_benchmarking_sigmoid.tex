                \begin{equation*}
                    S(x) = \frac{100}{1 + \exp\left(-\kappa \cdot \dfrac{x - \median(X)}{\IQR(X)}\right)}

                    S_{\text{inv}}(x) = 100 - S(x), \quad \text{for Debt/Equity \& Debt Ratio}
                \end{equation*}
                Rewards conservative balance sheets with higher health scores.
