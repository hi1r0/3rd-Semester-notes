                \fontsize{6.3pt}{8.2pt}\selectfont
                Quantifies exact point impact of each ratio on composite score:
                \begin{equation*}
                    \Delta S_i = W_k \cdot w_{k,i}' \times \big(S(x_i) - 50.0\big)
                \end{equation*}
                \textbf{\color{deepemerald}Attribution Directionality:}
                \begin{itemize}
                    \setlength{\itemsep}{1.5pt}
                    \item $\Delta S_i > 0$: Metric exceeds peer median, delivering positive alpha.
                    \item $\Delta S_i < 0$: Pinpoints the specific fundamental drag causing rating downgrades or operational underperformance.
                \end{itemize}
            \end{mathcard}
