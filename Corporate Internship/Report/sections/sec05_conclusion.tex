\section{Conclusion and Professional Reflections}

FinMetrics Suite successfully bridges rigorous statistical theory, machine learning, and enterprise financial software engineering. By replacing fragile Gaussian assumptions with high-breakdown non-parametric estimators (Tukey IQR, MAD-based Robust Z-Scores) and unsupervised Isolation Forests, the platform insulates peer benchmarking against market skewness and outlier masking. Median-anchored sigmoid normalization and dynamic weight redistribution further guarantee fair, transparent, and explainable credit and equity risk assessments.

The 6-week corporate internship at IQ Business Advisors provided hands-on experience in productionizing quantitative models for real-world client workflows. The project achieved all core learning objectives of MSQF 534 (CO1--CO5), demonstrating the fusion of academic financial mathematics with automated corporate advisory solutions.
