\section{Introduction and Organizational Context}

\subsection{Company Profile: IQ Business Advisors}
The 6-week corporate internship was completed at \textbf{IQ Business Advisors}, a strategic financial advisory firm founded in 2024 by Mr.~Naga Arjun and Mr.~Rajesh Kanan. The organization specializes in three core operational domains:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Auditing \& Process Optimization:} Streamlining internal controls, accounting systems, and statutory audits.
    \item \textbf{Strategic Financial Advisory:} Providing transaction diligence, portfolio valuation, and corporate restructuring.
    \item \textbf{Enterprise Analytical Automation:} Building custom quantitative workflows and decision-support algorithms.
\end{itemize}
Interns are embedded directly within live client delivery teams, translating corporate advisory needs into production software.

\subsection{Project Overview and Core Objectives}
\textbf{FinMetrics Suite} is an automated quantitative analysis platform developed to evaluate equities on the National Stock Exchange (NSE). Given any stock ticker, the engine retrieves filings, benchmarks the company against sector peers across 16 core financial ratios, and isolates univariate and joint structural anomalies using robust non-parametric statistics and machine learning.

The project fulfilled five core industrial and academic objectives:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Automate Ingestion \& Taxonomy Alignment:} Ingest exchange disclosures without manual spreadsheet extraction while harmonizing disparate accounting taxonomies across peer companies.
    \item \textbf{High-Breakdown Outlier Detection:} Replace fragile Gaussian estimators with non-parametric methods ($50\%$ breakdown point) to insulate peer distributions against severe skewness and outlier masking.
    \item \textbf{Median-Anchored Relative Benchmarking:} Normalize heterogeneous metric scales onto a unified 0--100 scale using sigmoid transformations anchored to sector medians and interquartile ranges.
    \item \textbf{Multidimensional Risk Synthesis:} Formulate an explainable Composite Health Score and a 5-tier financial risk classification separating opportunity drivers from distress factors.
    \item \textbf{Dual-Channel Enterprise Reporting:} Deploy a real-time Streamlit web dashboard alongside an automated, client-ready 6-tab Excel financial diagnostics workbook.
\end{enumerate}
