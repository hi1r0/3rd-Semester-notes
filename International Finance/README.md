## 📑 Curriculum Coverage: Units 1 – 5

1. **UNIT: 1 (9 Hours) — Financial Management in a Global Perspective & Monetary Systems**
   - Globalisation, cross-border financial flows, international trade trends, and India in the global economy.
   - Balance of Payments (BOP), International Monetary System, and overview of international financial markets.
   - Exchange rate determination and forecasting: Purchasing Power Parity (PPP), the Fisher Effect, and Interest Rate Parity (IRP).

2. **UNIT: 2 (9 Hours) — The Foreign Exchange Market & Currency Derivatives**
   - Structure, participants, transaction types, dealing mechanics, and exchange rate quotations.
   - Triangular and covered arbitrage, forward rates, evolution of exchange controls, and the Indian forex market.
   - Currency derivatives: Mechanics and hedging applications of currency futures and currency options.

3. **UNIT: 3 (9 Hours) — Foreign Exchange Exposure and Risk Management**
   - Identification, measurement, and active corporate management of tripartite FX exposure:
     - **Economic Exposure:** Operating cash flow sensitivities.
     - **Transaction Exposure:** Contractual payment and receipt hedges (forwards, money markets, options).
     - **Translation Exposure:** Balance sheet consolidation methods and accounting variance mitigation.

4. **UNIT: 4 (9 Hours) — Working Capital Management in a Multinational Context & Capital Markets**
   - Multinational cash management: Centralised vs. decentralised architectures, short-term borrowing and investing, cash transmission networks.
   - International equity investment: Risk-return profile, International CAPM (ICAPM), equity financing in global capital markets.
   - Long-term borrowing in international capital markets: Major market segments, international financing decisions, and international leasing.

5. **UNIT: 5 (9 Hours) — International Project Appraisal, Swaps, Accounting & Taxation**
   - International capital budgeting: Review of NPV, Adjusted Present Value (APV) framework, exchange rate risk adjustments, hurdle rates, and international joint ventures.
   - Financial swaps: Interest rate and currency swaps, comparative advantage motivations, applications, and valuation mechanics.
   - International accounting and taxation: Multi-currency transaction ledgers and accounting for foreign subsidiaries.
