\section[Sensitivity Analysis]{Sensitivity Analysis: Hindustan Unilever Break-Even Dynamics}

\subsection{Algebraic Derivation of the Break-Even Perpetual Growth Rate}
Because the Gordon Growth Model displays asymptotic convexity as $g \to \Ke$, the valuation of Hindustan Unilever (\inr 2,512 at $g = 12.0\%$ vs Market Price of \inr 1,973) is subjected to rigorous sensitivity analysis. We analytically solve for the \textbf{break-even growth rate ($\bm{g^*}$)} where intrinsic value exactly equates to the current market quotation:

\begin{equation}
    P_0 = \frac{\text{Proxy DPS} \times (1 + g)}{\Ke - g} \implies 1,973 = \frac{44.86\,(1 + g)}{0.14 - g}
\end{equation}

Expanding and solving for $g$:
\begin{align}
    1,973 \times (0.14 - g) = 44.86\,(1 + g) &\implies 276.22 - 1,973\,g = 44.86 + 44.86\,g \\
    231.36 = 2,017.86\,g &\implies g^* = \frac{231.36}{2,017.86} \approx \bm{11.46\%}
\end{align}

\begin{formulabox}[HUL Growth-Rate Decision Boundaries]
\centering\footnotesize
$\bm{g > 11.46\%}$: \textbf{Undervalued} \quad $\bm{\cdot}$ \quad
$\bm{g = 11.46\%}$: \textbf{Fairly Valued} \quad $\bm{\cdot}$ \quad
$\bm{g < 11.46\%}$: \textbf{Overvalued}
\end{formulabox}

\subsection{Cross-Sectional Growth Gap and Cost-of-Equity Stress Testing}
Sensitivity dynamics extend beyond HUL across the entire cross-section:
\begin{enumerate}[label=\textbf{\arabic*.}, topsep=2pt, itemsep=1.5pt, parsep=0pt]
    \item \textbf{Knife-Edge Valuation Boundaries:} While HUL displays a positive growth buffer of \textbf{$+0.53\,\text{pp}$} ($g = 12.00\%$ vs $g^* = 11.47\%$), two peers operate on razor-thin overvaluation thresholds: \textbf{Varun Beverages} is overvalued by a mere \textbf{$-0.26\,\text{pp}$} ($g^* = 12.26\%$) and \textbf{Britannia Industries} by \textbf{$-0.38\,\text{pp}$} ($g^* = 12.38\%$).
    \item \textbf{Discount-Rate Stress Testing ($\bm{\Ke = 13.0\%}$):} A 100-basis-point reduction in the cost of equity to $13.0\%$ lowers market-implied growth hurdles to $11.27\%$ for Varun and $11.39\%$ for Britannia. Consequently, \textbf{all three growth-capped enterprises flip to Undervalued}.
    \item \textbf{Structural Robustness:} The remaining seven companies face substantial fundamental deficits ($g - g^* \le -1.28\,\text{pp}$), remaining decisively overvalued across all plausible parameters. Thus, only \textbf{seven of the ten overvaluations are analytically robust}.
\end{enumerate}
