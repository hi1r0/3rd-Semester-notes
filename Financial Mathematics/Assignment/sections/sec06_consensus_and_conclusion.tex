\section[Academic Conclusion \& Formulas]{Academic Conclusion and Master Formula Reference}

\subsection{Strategic Synthesis of Empirical Findings}
\begin{enumerate}[label=\textbf{\arabic*.}, topsep=1pt, itemsep=0.5pt, parsep=0pt]
    \item \textbf{Tangible Floor Disconnect:} All ten enterprises trade at substantial multiples ($4.5\times$ to $43.4\times$) above tangible book value. FMCG valuations reflect off-balance-sheet intangible moats (brand equity, negative working capital cycles, distribution scale) rather than physical asset liquidation worth.
    \item \textbf{Dividend Yield Deficit:} With dividend yields spanning $0.91\%$ to $4.45\%$, zero firms meet the $14.0\%$ cost-of-equity hurdle, proving that secondary markets price in long-term earnings compounding rather than current income.
    \item \textbf{Consensus Nuance and Frontier Classification:} While mechanical 2-of-3 majority rule classifies \textbf{nine firms as Overvalued} and \textbf{HUL as Mixed / Neutral}, TBV and raw yield systematically penalize growth franchises by design. Accounting for discount-rate sensitivity ($\Ke = 13\%$), \textbf{only seven overvaluations are robust}; HUL, Varun Beverages, and Britannia sit on razor-thin valuation boundaries.
\end{enumerate}
