## 📑 Core Topics Covered

1. **Unit I: International Financial Architecture & Balance of Payments**
   - World Bank Group (IBRD, IDA, IFC, MIGA, ICSID) and governance structures.
   - International Monetary Fund (IMF) statutory mandate, surveillance, lending facilities, and UN SDGs.
   - Balance of Payments (BOP) dynamics: Current, Capital, and Financial Account disequilibrium and policy toolkits.
   - Special Drawing Rights (SDR) basket weighting, allocations, and SDR interest rate mechanism.
   - Asian Development Bank (ADB) comparison with the IMF.
   - Internationalization process: Evolutionary stages, expansion drivers, and foreign market entry strategies (OLI paradigm).
   - Emerging systemic challenges in international finance.
2. **Unit II: The Foreign Exchange Market**
   - Market structure, wholesale vs. retail tiers, two-way quotes, bid-ask spreads, and cross rates.
   - SWIFT and CHIPS international settlement and clearing architectures.
   - Covered Interest Parity (CIP) and forward margins/swap points.
   - Indian merchant rates under FEDAI guidelines: TT Buying, Bill Buying, TT Selling, and Bill Selling rates with standardized pro-forma accounting ledgers.
   - Ready cross rates, foreign demand draft cancellations, and forward exchange delivery horizons under FEDAI Rule 7.
3. **Swiss FDI Empirical Monograph (2015–2024)**
   - Analysis of SNB capital stocks, flows, investment income, and Ultimate Beneficial Owner (UBO) conduit dynamics.
   - Sectoral allocations, global labor multipliers of Swiss multinational enterprises, and econometric determinants.
