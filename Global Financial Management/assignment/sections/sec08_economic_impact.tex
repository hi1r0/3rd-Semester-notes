\section{Economic Impact of FDI}

The interplay between foreign investment inside Switzerland and the overseas operations of Swiss multinationals stimulates domestic employment, accelerates gross capital formation, and amplifies total factor productivity.

\subsection{Domestic Parent Employment and Macroeconomic Spillovers}

In 2024, multinational parent entities directly employed \textbf{$560,964$ persons} in Switzerland across high-wage headquarters, laboratories, and specialized production facilities (Table~\ref{tab:domestic_parent_employment}).

\noindent\textbf{Core Spillover Channels:}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em, itemsep=1.5pt]
    \item \textbf{Capital Formation:} Direct investment contributes an estimated \textbf{$15\pct$ to $20\pct$} of annual Swiss Gross Fixed Capital Formation (GFCF), directed heavily toward automated cleanrooms and IT infrastructure.
    \item \textbf{Trade Integration:} Multinational enterprises account for \textbf{$70\pct$ of total Swiss exports}, utilizing global affiliate networks to distribute high-margin pharmaceuticals and precision machinery.
    \item \textbf{GDP Contribution:} Direct operational activities and knowledge spillovers contribute an estimated \textbf{$1.5\pct$ to $3.0\pct$} to annual Swiss real GDP growth.
\end{itemize}
