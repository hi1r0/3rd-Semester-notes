\begin{remarkbox}[The $4.43\times$ Global Labor Multiplier]
In 2024, Swiss MNEs employed \textbf{2,483,500 people abroad} across $21,265$ subsidiaries. Compared to the $560,964$ domestic personnel employed by Swiss parent companies in Switzerland, Swiss enterprise sustains an \textbf{overseas-to-domestic labor multiplier of $4.43\times$}. For every employee at a corporate headquarters in Switzerland, Swiss MNEs support more than four workers in global manufacturing, clinical trials, and distribution worldwide.
\end{remarkbox}

\subsection{Geographic Distribution and Value Chain Governance}

The regional dispersion of the $21,265$ foreign subsidiaries in 2024 comprises: \textbf{Europe}: $12,892$ ($60.6\pct$); \textbf{Asia}: $3,184$ ($15.0\pct$); \textbf{North America}: $2,443$ ($11.5\pct$); \textbf{Central/South America}: $1,497$ ($7.0\pct$); \textbf{Africa}: $834$ ($3.9\pct$); and \textbf{Oceania}: $415$ ($2.0\pct$).

\begin{mnecase}{Nestlé's Decentralized Value Chain Governance}{nestle_architecture}
\textbf{Nestlé S.A.} operates over 340 production factories across 77 nations, decentralizing packaging and local sourcing while concentrating fundamental nutritional R\&D and global trademark ownership at its Swiss parent headquarters in Vevey.
\end{mnecase}
