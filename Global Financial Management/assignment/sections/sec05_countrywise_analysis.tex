\section{Country-wise FDI Analysis}

Evaluating bilateral geographic allocations requires distinguishing between the \textbf{Immediate Investor} (direct creditor jurisdiction) and the \textbf{Ultimate Beneficial Owner (UBO)}, which identifies the ultimate controlling corporate parent.

\begin{remarkbox}[Unmasking the American Capital Preponderance]
While the Netherlands ($26.4\pct$) and Luxembourg ($15.2\pct$) appear as the largest immediate creditor nations, their share collapses by over $85\pct$ under UBO accounting. Conversely, the \textbf{United States surges from $18.2\pct$ to $40.0\pct$ ($\CHF\,370.7\bn$)}, demonstrating that American multinationals heavily route European investments through Benelux Special Purpose Entities (SPEs) prior to deployment into Switzerland.
\end{remarkbox}
